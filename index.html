Safety Rules & Terms and Conditions

Please read all applicable zone rules and commercial terms carefully before participating.

Go-Karting Track Safety & Driving Rules

GO-KART SAFETY WARNING: Go-karts have rotating and moving parts. Hair longer than shoulder-length must be secured above the shoulder, and all personal items (loose clothing, scarves, dupattas, jewelry, phones, etc.) must be secured and contained inside the seating area; otherwise, serious injury or death may occur.
  • All single-kart drivers must meet the minimum track height requirement of in ( cm) tall.
  • For double-seat go-karts, passengers must be at least 40 in (101.6 cm) tall and possess upper-body control, with the driver being at least 18 years of age or the parent/legal guardian of the passenger.
  • Any HAIR longer than shoulder-length MUST be tied and secured above the shoulder; approved helmets must be worn properly fastened if provided.
  • All LOOSE CLOTHING must be fastened and secured before entering the track area.
  • Closed SHOES and a SHIRT must be worn at all times while in the track area.
  • EATING, DRINKING, AND SMOKING / VAPING are strictly prohibited in go-karts and anywhere inside the track or pit area.
  • Both hands must remain on the steering wheel and both feet inside the go-kart floorboard at all times unless instructed otherwise by a track attendant.
  • Anyone suspected of being under the influence of ALCOHOL OR DRUGS will be denied entry immediately.
  • BUMPING, RAMMING, OR INTENTIONAL CONTACT between go-karts or against track barriers is strictly prohibited.
  • When entering the pit area, go-karts must be slowed down completely and riders must remain seated and await attendants' instructions.
  • Seat belts / safety harnesses must be properly worn and fastened at all times while seated in a go-kart.
  • Drivers and passengers must remain seated inside the go-kart at all times. If a kart stops or trouble arises, remain seated and raise one hand to alert an attendant — never step onto an active track.
  • Any misconduct, reckless driving, or failure to obey attendant signals will result in immediate removal from the track area. Disqualification applies on the 1st warning without exception.

Soft Play & Trampoline Arena Safety Rules

TRAMPOLINE & SOFT PLAY WARNING: Jumping and climbing carry inherent risk of sprains, fractures, or impact injury. Never attempt flips or head-first dives unless specifically authorized, and always land on both feet or your seated bottom.
  • GRIP SOCKS MANDATORY: Non-slip trampoline grip socks must be worn at all times inside the trampoline & soft play area. Bare feet, street shoes, sandals, and socks without grip are prohibited.
  • ONE PERSON PER TRAMPOLINE MAT: Only one jumper is allowed per trampoline bed/square at a time. Double-bouncing another person is strictly prohibited.
  • NO FLIPS OR DANGEROUS STUNTS: Somersaults, backflips, inverted maneuvers, and head-first diving into foam pits or ball pools are prohibited.
  • EMPTY POCKETS & NO SHARP OBJECTS: Remove all jewelry, watches, belts, buckles, keys, coins, pens, and unsecured eyewear/phones before entering.
  • AGE, HEIGHT & SUPERVISION: Children must use age-appropriate zones (toddler soft play vs. main trampolines). Parents/guardians are solely responsible for actively supervising their children at all times.
  • NO ROUGH PLAY OR CLIMBING ON PADS/NETS: Pushing, tackling, wrestling, running across pads, hanging on basketball rims, or climbing safety netting/walls is prohibited.
  • NO SITTING OR LYING ON MATS: Do not sit, lie down, or rest on active trampoline beds; step onto padded walkways to rest.
  • NO FOOD, DRINKS, OR GUM: Food, beverages, and chewing gum are strictly prohibited inside the soft play and trampoline arena.
  • OBEY COURT MONITORS: Failure to follow attendant whistle signals or rules results in immediate removal without refund.

Bowling Alley Safety & Lane Rules

BOWLING LANE WARNING: Bowling lanes beyond the foul line are heavily oiled and extremely slippery. Never cross the foul line, and keep hands and fingers clear of the ball return mechanism.
  • BOWLING SHOES MANDATORY: Proper bowling shoes must be worn on the lane approaches. Street shoes, heels, bare feet, or wet soles are prohibited for safety and lane protection.
  • DO NOT CROSS THE FOUL LINE: Stepping past the foul line onto the oiled lane surface is dangerous and strictly prohibited.
  • SAFE BALL HANDLING & PINSETTER CLEARANCE: Choose a ball weight appropriate for your age and strength. Never reach inside the ball return opening, and always wait until the pinsetter sweep bar has lifted completely before bowling.
  • ONE BOWLER AT A TIME & LANE COURTESY: Only one person may step onto the approach to bowl at a time; yield to bowlers on adjacent lanes and keep children behind the seating step when not bowling.
  • NO LOFTING OR RECKLESS THROWING: Lofting balls high onto the lane, throwing multiple balls at once, throwing balls backwards, or swinging balls recklessly is strictly prohibited.
  • NO FOOD OR DRINKS ON APPROACH: Keep all food and beverages in the seating/table area — never on the wooden/synthetic approach area (spills cause dangerous slips).
  • EQUIPMENT CARE: Rental bowling shoes and house balls must be returned to the counter/racks after play.

Participation is not recommended for individuals with heart, neck, back, or spine problems, recent surgery, bone/joint injuries, or for pregnant women. Participation is strictly at your own risk.

  • Fun Strikes Entertainment LLP / Fun Strike! Game Zone reserves the right to refuse service or deny entry to any guest for safety, medical, or operational reasons.
